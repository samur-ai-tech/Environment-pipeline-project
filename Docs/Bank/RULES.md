# Bank rules

Effective 2026-10-17, revised at Aviv's request. This page contains the standing bank rules; it grants no execution authority.
A documented procedure does not resume a stopped task or expand the user's current authorized scope.

## What correct means

An item is correct for a named capability and area when its identity/bindings and applicable measurements are established, an executable consumer supports that use, area policy permits it, and the requested output passes its scoped checks. Saved environment acceptance also requires visible correct output, named saves, fresh reopen and ordinary regeneration without unwanted duplicates. A scope-complete offline delivery remains distinct from that integration.

Distinguish technical mechanisms supported by Houdini Engine, declared project contracts, and acceptance evidence. Facing, anchors, floor limits, areas and holds are project contracts; retain useful validation without presenting them as universal Houdini requirements. Report inventory, adaptation, consumer support, area eligibility, native output, persistence and visual acceptance separately in existing records; this is not a new runtime schema.

## Authority and task scope

- Follow the user's latest explicit instruction and the applicable [project instructions](../../AGENTS.md). This guide does not appoint or replace the global manager.
- Before implementation, establish the actual owner, assignment, writable files/packages/maps and required review. A copied brief, old PID, released lease or saved JSON record is not fresh permission.
- Keep bank identity/eligibility ownership, shared Houdini integration, kit authoring and native map operation distinct. Coordinate changes that cross those scopes with the responsible owners.
- Git, issue-tracker actions, messaging other chats, automation changes and additional work scopes are not implied by organizing documentation.

## Preserve assets and identities

- Reuse existing AssetIds, row identities, native ObjectPaths and source provenance. Deduplicate before importing or registering; an existing asset may need a consumer connection rather than another import.
- Preserve originals, native materials/textures, dependencies, Blueprint/component behavior, source packages and earlier HDA revisions. Do not move referenced assets or replace vendor content to tidy folders.
- Read each asset's current canonical row and release/hold fields. Registration, a copied kit table or a consumer catalog does not release a hold. Do not maintain a second frozen list of held IDs here.
- Resolve the current native readback through [Files and bank state](FILES_AND_STATE.md#shared-bank-snapshot). The 8 October readback/handoff is historical publication evidence; the 18 October user exclusions supersede its eligibility snapshot. Verify current bytes before a later write.
- Preserve rights and acquisition evidence and recorded restrictions. A technical pass is not a new rights or selection approval.
- Keep geographic/Houdini sources under `C:/Users/USER/Documents/Pipeline Environment`. Shared native content belongs under its existing registered paths; retained area output belongs under `/Game/Project/Areas/{AreaId}/`.
- Archive superseded instructions and retain primary evidence. Asset deletion, backup restoration or cleanup requires its own explicit scope and reference checks.

## Editor ownership and saving

- Inspect current processes, the last [editor ownership record](../../Staging/ModelBank/EDITOR_OWNER.txt), target world, dirty packages and other active assignments before editor work.
- One writer owns each package and map. Parallel authoring requires disjoint scopes and PID/process-start-verified endpoints and records; use the [parallel record specification](../../Staging/ParallelEditors/README.md).
- For generation, follow the single-editor window in the later [new-area playbook](../../Staging/Playbooks/NEW_ENVIRONMENT_PLAYBOOK.md) and satisfy the current authorized launcher/runtime checks. Earlier parallel-editor records do not waive generation safeguards.
- Bank writes overlap a generation that reads the bank. Do not change shared profiles, source, tables, HDA or dependencies during that run.
- Preserve other sessions and unsaved buffers. Never close, save, discard or overwrite another owner's work or save a stale loaded copy over a newer package.
- Save only exact owned, reviewed packages and the named map. Never use Save All or `save_assets([])`. Record partial saves and failures honestly; memory rollback is not disk rollback.
- Follow the authorized failure/recovery procedure. Do not clear safeguards, rewrite expected hashes, invent leases or retry against another writer to force a pass.

## Retained route and consumer boundaries

- Keep the retained route: validated geographic source → owned Houdini HDA → official Houdini Engine → native Unreal assets/output → scoped Bake and persistence.
- Keep reusable HDA logic in its retained PythonModule, not only in `hou.session`. Use reviewed source revisions and the existing official Engine lifecycle; a Houdini authoring connection is separate from native Engine execution.
- A bank row supplies identity, references and eligibility metadata. Its placement or assembly consumer must exist and support the requested role; rows and imports alone do not establish a usable connection.
- Whole-building placement and modular assembly are different lanes. Respect measured units, axes, pivots, offsets, source proportions, material slots, fit envelopes and declared growth/height capabilities. Do not infer floors, use or host support without evidence.
- Ordinary native-reference output may emit asset paths, transforms and material bindings without materializing every native mesh in Houdini. Retain real hash-bound geometry where the selected source-geometry route requires it.
- Before repair, search affected callers/builders and list dependent tests/self-checks, generated files, native bindings, manifests, registries, profiles, HDA source/imported definitions and evidence. Change the smallest complete dependency set; changing a builder does not update generated assets.
- PCG vegetation owns Unreal scattering; ground owns its material/painting contract; roads own their assigned provider and geometry. Their catalogs may reuse bank identities but must preserve canonical holds and their own placement rules.
- The [dated consumer map](../../Staging/BankSimplification_2026-10-07/CONSUMER_MAP.md) explains those interfaces. Its counts and planned transitions are historical observations, not current activation instructions.
- A table change must preserve the authorized release and hold policy, recompute the logical catalog digest for the reviewed row set, and synchronize every affected current profile through its owner. A source-file hash is not a substitute for the logical catalog digest.

## Verification and acceptance

- Validity flags record claims, not measurements. Bind applicable bounds/contact/facing evidence to the exact native revision/hash, method and measured result. Check its physical meaning at adaptation/publication; missing proof is not proof of a wrong model. Do not substitute invented valid metadata or add a whole-bank remeasurement to every cook.
- Report preparation, offline validation, native import/load, registration, enabled selection, actual placement, visible appearance, named save, fresh reopen and repeat regeneration separately.
- Use the real retained authorized result. Do not introduce disposable demonstrations, alternate generators, forced family admission or weakened geometry/completeness checks to obtain a screenshot.
- Preserve original appearance and relevant component/material behavior. Unknown native properties, dependencies, unsupported recipes and unverified source semantics remain explicit gaps.
- A bounded import/repair can be complete without proving a kit or city connection. Acceptance of that integration requires real output, original appearance, exact named saves, fresh process/map verification and ordinary regeneration without duplicate prior output; Cook success, counts or matching hashes alone do not establish it.
- A stopped or incomplete candidate remains so until its actual owner receives new applicable authority and supplies the missing evidence. Documentation changes do not promote candidates or resume work.

## Keep these documents usable

- Maintain one current page per topic in this guide. Update its as-of date, scope, responsible owner, supported procedure/version, exact evidence and remaining gaps when the underlying state changes.
- Link to canonical rows, contracts, tools and immutable receipts instead of copying their entire contents into another “current” document. Keep general procedure separate from kit-specific measurements and dated run records.
- Mark a superseded page as historical and point to its successor. Preserve its original evidence; do not leave two unlabeled competing procedures.
- Keep commands tied to their supported tool/input version. Replace stale current pointers and broken links; do not silently substitute a new baseline into an old frozen transaction.
- After a bank change, the responsible owner updates the affected current status/procedure page and links its new receipt. Offline supplier handoffs remain delivery-time records; later native outcomes belong to the receiving operator's evidence.

## Rules

- One row per issue. IDs are `ISS-NNN`. They are never reused or renumbered. New issues are appended at the end of the table.
- Fields: ID | opened | area/system | issue | evidence (path:line) | priority | owner | closure criterion | status | closed by.
- Priority:
  - **P1**: blocks safe work;
  - **P2**: risk of wrong work;
  - **P3**: cosmetic.
- Owner: Aviv, unless he assigns an agent to the issue (`GR:7`, `GR:54`).
- Status is `open` or `closed`. A row is closed only when its closure criterion is met or Aviv decides it. "closed by" then names the record and the date.
- A closed row stays in the table. If part of a closed item is still open, that part gets its own open row with the same source ID.
- Seeding rules used on 2026-10-19:
  - Checklist items keep their RC id.
  - Priority follows the item's severity: S2 → P2, S3 → P3.
  - Part A items have no severity in their titles. For those, the highest severity in their "Closes:" line is used, so every open Part A item is P2.
  - No item is P1, because the audit recorded no S1 finding (`CL:16`).
  - The closure criterion of a checklist item is its acceptance check in short form. The full text is at the cited `CL` line.
  - Some items are already decided in `GR` but their acceptance check is not yet met. Those rows stay open and say "decided `GR:nn`; remaining: …".
  - Sealed-code backlog items 1-14 are P2. Each awaits Aviv's approve or reject, one by one (`GR:115`). Item 15 is not listed, because Aviv already validated it (`GR:107`).

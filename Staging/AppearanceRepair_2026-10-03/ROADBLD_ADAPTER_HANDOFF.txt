Lifecycle and failure boundary
The staged phase native_generated_validated_pending_save has saved=false and a live token bound to PID/lease/revision/map. HDA suppression consumes only that exact validated transaction, retains pedestrian/track surfaces, and exports the original contract. commit refuses any receipt without matching provider/area/source/centerline/sourceIDs/pieceIDs/count/token and confirmed suppressed vehicle surfaces. Only after completed HDA Bake/old scoped HDA cleanup does commit remove prior native actors listed in their receipt and save named dependencies/map. The root's saved-contract persistence/final named map save follows. There is no intermediate map save with both visible road providers.

If generation fails, the previous saved delivery receipt/map are preserved. Exact staged paths are recorded and no broad rollback or Save All occurs. Root must reconcile those paths before retry; the adapter rejects unrecorded owned staging. Native runtime may enforce a larger radius or produce full-width road caps/sidewalks beyond the map or building pads.

Units, axes and anchors
Native measurements are Unreal centimetres with Z up. Blender source measurements
are evaluated object-world coordinates multiplied by scene.unit_settings.scale_length,
reported in metres. Existing archive .blend files and FBX exports are retained;
no arbitrary axis rotation, 100x scaling or origin relocation is performed.
The external.H0633 material-only derivative reuses the exact geometry/UV/slot-face
assignment of external.T1157 and exports with axes read from its original FBX.
Blender source bounds must NOT be copied to native XYZ runtime columns until native
import readback establishes the export/import axis conversion for that asset.
Houdini uses metres and Y up; official Engine converts to Unreal centimetres and
Z up. The retained consumer compensates native horizontal AABB centre and AnchorZCm
through its yaw quaternion. Native +X/+Y/-X/-Y ForwardAxis controls yaw correction.
A longest horizontal dimension supplies geographic alignment, not a proven facade
or seat-facing direction. Ground minimum vertex is a measured anchor candidate,
not proof of a contact surface, wheel contact, foliage root or material appearance.
Whole buildings retain 1:1 scale, measured footprint envelope, source height fit
and final terrain support. Do not stretch a complete building to fill a pad.

Geometry, collision, materials and dependencies
Preserve original vertices, UV layers, custom normals and object transforms.
Preserve exact material slot order per object. Do not merge unrelated subobjects
or count a compound source scene as one verified whole building.
UCX/UBX/USP/UCP collision meshes and LOD1+ meshes are retained but excluded from
LOD0 render measurements and thumbnails. Collision helpers do not require UVs.
Native measurements distinguish render bounds from exact source-vertex bounds.
Use the actual native LOD and collision records; do not infer performance from
triangle count alone. No LOD/Nanite regeneration is performed in this phase.
Source material maps retain their exact channel, texture path, UV scale and UV
offset. Reported ORM maps use R=AO, G=roughness, B=metallic. These are source report
semantics, not a new native shader validation. Missing files are never replaced
by similarly named guessed textures. Native original material closure and project
instancing overrides must be reviewed separately; a repaired override can differ
from an original material's historical unresolved paths.

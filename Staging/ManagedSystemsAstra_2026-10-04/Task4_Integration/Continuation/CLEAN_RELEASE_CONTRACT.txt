actual release. bank_clean_handoff_receipt is the exact absolute receipt path;

manager_thread_id, operator_thread_id, released_editor_pid, map;

Landscape ownership is a separate manager-reviewed immutable receipt selected by
landscape_ownership_receipt and landscape_ownership_sha256. Required fields: map,
map_sha256, source_sha256, hda_actor_path, actor_paths, official_actor_paths,
baked_actor_paths. All actor entries are exact full map-local paths. actor_paths
must equal union(official_actor_paths,baked_actor_paths); official_actor_paths must
be nonempty. Native helper result must equal that official set, live Landscape
membership must equal actor_paths, and last-success baked paths intersected with
actor_paths must equal baked_actor_paths. Missing/extra actors, empty helper output,
changed map/source/HDA or ownership reject. This never grants cleanup permission.
Initial Shujaiya expectation contains both known saved Landscapes. After ordinary
successful replacement select a NEW receipt bound to the saved map hash and exact
singleton. Rimal has its own expectation. Do not weaken unreadable-identity gates.

Native table receipt selection is explicit and immutable. Capture takes
--native-table-selection <exact-Task4-staging-selection-file>. Selection fields:
manager_thread_id, source_sha256, csv_sha256, receipt, receipt_sha256,
native_readback, native_readback_sha256. Receipt must record saved=true, rows,
digest, table; readback must be actual exported native rows matching source/CSV.
Receipt may be a bank apply or supplier REGISTRATION_APPLIED/APPLIED result; no
filename is hardcoded. Preserve immutable transaction receipt and actual native
readback; never use an old bank receipt after supplier appends. A supplier receipt
without corresponding actual native readback cannot pass this contract.
STARTUP_ACTIVATION.native_table_selection_sha256 explicitly designates the exact
selection accepted by the manager. Preparation's current archived bank evidence
is only a candidate selection, not approval or a live table inspection.

Assignment/transfer are dynamic documents. Snapshot stores observed hashes for
audit but verifies current manager, exact Task4 operator, TRANSFERRED_TO_SUCCESSOR,
successor ID, and exact original-map set. Unrelated status changes do not authorize
different identities, replace transfer checks, or reset the source seal.

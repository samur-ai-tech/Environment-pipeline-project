## What has been verified

1. **Historical kit construction:** Each five-part kit was adapted or built in Blender, exported as FBX, imported into old `GIS_WorldBLD`, assigned a CityBLD Building Style, and tested in 10/10 rectangular building regeneration cases after a fresh editor reopen. The reports limit these results to rectangular exterior forms; they do not establish arbitrary footprint, interior, slope, material, or performance quality. These parts were not produced by a blanket process that cut every complete building in the bank. [documented: [Astra Result_Report](<C:/Users/USER/Documents/Unreal Projects/GIS_WorldBLD/SourceData/CityBLD_Astra_MinKit_20260924/Result_Report.md>), [Terre Result_Report](<C:/Users/USER/Documents/Unreal Projects/GIS_WorldBLD/SourceData/CityBLD_Terre_RuralKit_20260924/Result_Report.md>)]
2. **Historical geographic use:** An old TwinBLD/CityBLD customizer using Astra generated 51 visible buildings with walls/materials and 15 road actors represented by 17 nonempty surfaces. Save, fresh-process reopen, and rerun had no duplicate source IDs. Five source building features lacked independent representation, maximum recorded footprint error was 302.37 cm, and terrain/road/vegetation and final acceptance remained open. This is **not** a Houdini Engine result. [documented: [old geographic pilot](<C:/Users/USER/Documents/Unreal Projects/GIS_WorldBLD/Saved/RootCause20260924/REPORT_HE.md>)]
3. **Current candidate and technical review:** The v7 index and final disposition record were independently counted: 1,215 candidate rows; **1,213 `reserve`, two `exclude`, zero `qualified`**. Every v7 row is `qualified=no`, `connected=no`, `generated=no`, `technical_eligible=false`, and `generation_enabled=false`. Fifteen candidates have measured native bounds/material paths; 75 of 930 current rows have saved partial native views. These observations do not prove placement, role fit, performance, or acceptance. [measured: [v7 CSV](BANK_CANDIDATE_INDEX_2026-09-29.csv), [disposition record](BANK_1215_DISPOSITION_RECORD_2026-09-29.csv); documented: [decision sheet](BANK_DECISION_SHEET_2026-09-29.EN.md)]

The disabled synthetic catalog test also produced zero instances; an enabled-row test failed the HDA cook. This is not a successful eligible-model placement test. [documented: [Houdini v002 receipt](<C:/Users/USER/Documents/Pipeline Environment/Houdini/GeoCity_city_v002/RESULT.md>)]

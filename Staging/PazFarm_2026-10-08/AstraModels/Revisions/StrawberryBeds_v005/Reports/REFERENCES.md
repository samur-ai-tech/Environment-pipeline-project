## Selected appearance

Use **dark charcoal-black matte mulch**, guided by the Huelva photograph: dusty film, gentle wrinkles, rounded shoulders, sloping sides and irregular soil-covered edges. Use **young leafy plugs**, guided by UC ANR, rather than Huelva's nearly bare crowns. Leaves should be small, serrated and trifoliate, with varied tilt and natural muted green. Broad areas of film remain exposed. **No fruit or flowers.**

The UC white-film photograph supplies plant morphology, relative scale and irrigation/bed-end details only. Its white colour and multi-row arrangement are not the selected design. The target **0.25 m mound height, 0.8 m top width and 1.1 m base width come from the brief**, not measurements inferred from photographs. The brief also controls tile length, row spacing, holes, skirt and material roughness (at least 0.85).

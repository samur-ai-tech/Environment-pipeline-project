# StrawberryBeds v005 photographic references

Collected and visually inspected before modelling, 2026-10-11. Four selected photographs are retained unchanged at their published image URLs' native resolution. No crop, retouch, recolouring, upscaling or synthetic image generation was used. These are appearance references, not asset textures; original photo copyrights remain with their owners. No modelling was performed by this reference task.

## Appearance recommendation

Use dusty charcoal-black mulch with gently rounded shoulders, sloping sides and an irregular buried soil edge, guided by reference 1. Use separate small green young plugs from 2–3, with variable leaf tilt and a visible dark soil opening from 4. Keep broad areas of film exposed and omit fruit. The brief's dimensions, stagger, missing plants, skirt and material roughness remain the modelling contract; the photographs guide surface and form, not a new layout.

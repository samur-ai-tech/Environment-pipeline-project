# Aleppo pines v003

Three original Pinus halepensis variants at 8/11/15 m, with 6/8/10 m crowns. All six FBXs preserve the trunk-base origin.

LOD0 has solid needle prisms over closed clump hulls. LOD3 retains the corresponding trunk, branches and the same 23 clump centers; the hulls expand by 6.5% before envelope fitting to stand in for removed outer needles. All crown geometry is opaque; there are no cards or billboards.

The three PBR materials are shared. Needle and hull roughness is 0.96, specular 0.08; no wind, collision or alpha fading. Normals are OpenGL +Y. BaseColor PNG files were reloaded as sRGB for the previews. Geometry validation checks finite positions/UV0, no zero-area triangles, and exactly two incident polygons per edge of every closed primitive.

This is offline source artwork. Root supplies fresh-FBX round trips and fixed-camera distance evidence; Unreal/HLOD verification remains with the receiving operator.
